## What Is RaidFi?

RaidFi is a **contribution intelligence and reputation infrastructure layer** built for Web3 ecosystems.

It is not a rewards farming platform. It is not a marketing tool. It is not another engagement scheme.

RaidFi transforms on-chain and off-chain participation into **structured, verifiable contribution data** - and turns that data into **portable reputation**.

---

## The Problem

Web3 relies on community participation for distribution, awareness, and network effects.

But right now, contribution is:

- Unstructured
- Unverifiable
- Fragmented
- Easily manipulated

Projects cannot tell the difference between real contributors and noise generators. Contributors have no way to prove their track record. Engagement metrics are inflated and meaningless.

This creates **inefficiency, distrust, and capital misallocation** at scale.

---

## What RaidFi Does

RaidFi formalizes contribution as **structured data** rather than vague social proof.

```
Contribution → Tracking → Verification → Reputation → Access
```

The platform operates across four interconnected layers:

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| **Contribution Tracking** | Tracks on-chain and off-chain activity with pattern detection |
| **Reputation Engine** | Converts behavioral history into portable, manipulation-resistant scores |
| **Analytics & Intelligence** | Gives projects real contributor data instead of inflated metrics |
| **On-Chain Contribution Pools** | Optional, transparent pools for verified, high-signal contributors |

## The Reputation Layer

Reputation in RaidFi is not a score you game. It is a **behavioral credibility record**.

It factors in:

- **Consistency** - sustained contribution over time
- **Quality weighting** - not all actions are equal
- **Verified participation** - on-chain attestation where applicable
- **Impact patterns** - what actually moved the needle

Reputation decays for inactivity or abuse. It cannot be inflated through spam. It is wallet-linked and portable.

> Contribution history becomes an asset class of credibility.

---

## Attack Vector Awareness

RaidFi is designed with abuse resistance from the start.

| Vector | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Sybil attacks | Wallet-linked behavior history |
| Engagement inflation | Pattern detection and quality weighting |
| Coordinated manipulation | Reputation decay + cross-signal verification |

This is not an afterthought. It is core to the architecture.

## Raid Teams

Raid Teams are structured coordination groups for on-chain contribution pools.

They are **opt-in**, not mandatory.

Teams allow:
- Collective reputation stacking
- Performance benchmarking
- Project-level structured coordination

Over time, teams build **track records** - creating a meta-layer of contribution credibility that goes beyond individual scores.
